Process

From file to part, step by step

You always know where your order is. Each stage has a clear owner, a defined output and a timeline.

01 / DFM
Manufacturability review

We check your model for wall thickness, radii, tool access and tolerance realism, and send you a written DFM note within 24 hours. Changes are suggested before money moves, not after.

02 / QUOTE
Itemized proposal

Price, lead time, tolerance assumptions and finish options, line by line. No hidden setup fees, no surprises at invoicing.

03 / MACHINING
Production with checkpoints

Programs are verified against your model, material is confirmed, and in-process checks run on a fixed schedule. Photos at key stages on request.

04 / INSPECTION
Measure twice, ship once

A first-article report covers critical dimensions. CMM or manual inspection per your drawing callouts; records are kept on file.

05 / FINISHING
Surface and assembly

Anodizing, plating, bead blast or powder coat, then assembly and functional checks where the part needs it.

06 / SHIP
Packaged with proof

Parts are protected, certificates and reports are included, and you get a tracked shipping update. Repeat orders reuse the approved setup.
